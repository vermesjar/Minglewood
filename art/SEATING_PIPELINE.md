# Generated seating: art, physical authorship, and evidence

Design Lab runs the seating pipeline automatically after beauty generation. A user should not paint masks, move sitting offsets, or approve a boolean review stamp. Every new mapped seat needs both exact numerical evidence and independently recorded visual-quality evidence before publication.

1. Compile the final proxy against the final source drawings and declaration. Mechanical edits preserve existing source maps and fail if their bindings are stale. A new beauty take deliberately starts new source annotation.
2. Export all four resolved source views. The source-identity provider sees original beauty, physical part projections, and all-view context; it does not see a tested avatar mask or expected winner. Cached proposals bind source bytes, anchors, geometry, and producer version. Uncertainty remains recorded.
3. For a one-tile, single-contact floor seat with a wrapped bank, `seat_curved_generate.py` authors one shared smooth support well and rolled rim. The same deterministic profile search runs for every asset. Per-view correspondence is bounded and measured: at most seven source pixels of displacement, positive Jacobian at least .08, maximum stretch three, and support-contact drift at most .05 source pixel. Failure is explicit, never filled from a neighboring depth pixel.
4. `seat-curved-author.ts` validates complete four-view v2 depth maps against the exact source art and mechanical model. These maps describe new authored intent; they do not claim a unique 3D object can be recovered from a painted sprite. Rigid seats retain semantic v1 maps. Neither kind is considered approved at this stage.
5. Capture the actual `WorldView` for every facing, review outfit, and cushion. Capture receipts bind the renderer sources and exact files. The original avatar export contains unmasked RGBA, ownership, continuous anatomy, and original construction provenance. It excludes the tested mask and masked avatar.
6. Produce independent expectations. For authored curves, `seat_authored_review.py` ray-intersects the declared profile and correspondence independently in Python; it never reads the runtime depth buffer as an oracle. `seat_body_reference.py` derives body surfaces from original anatomy and construction. Ordinary semantic seats use the separate source-only reviewer. Unresolved pixels cannot pass.
7. The exact verifier requires every opaque overlap, unchanged visible avatar colors, no injected avatar pixels, original pixels outside furniture preserved, and independent final-canvas recomposition. A matching numerical result is implementation parity, not aesthetic approval.
8. `seat_visual_review.py` independently checks the entire resulting seat and body in every required context. Its source-bound sheets and enlarged risks expose enclosed upholstery pixels, detached body fragments, and questionable support contact. Every risk needs a recorded decision. A model provider can perform this review through the existing budget guard; local AI-agent reviews are labeled as agents and require two distinct complete records. Rejected or uncertain cases remain unpublished.
9. `seat_motion_review.py` installs the exact draft only in an isolated browser candidate room, records real approach/sitting/exit interactions for all required directions, outfits, and cushions, and checks byte invariants against the immutable settled expectations. Invariants alone do not approve transition aesthetics. Transition visual evidence is a separate requirement; pending or failed motion review cannot mean the generated seat is ready to publish.
10. `seat_wardrobe_review.py` derives exact sampled poses and leg geometry from the verified settled captures and every verified movement frame, adds the four natural seating styles and standing/crouching baseline, and runs the current catalog's body/top/bottom/shoe cross-product plus each remaining item individually. Every opaque original body pixel needs finite depth. The shared publication gate independently derives the required contexts and identities and refuses missing, stale, incomplete, or failing receipts. Studio shows this clothing coverage separately from four-outfit visual approval. Unsupported accessory cross-products, palettes and future clothes are not implicitly approved.

The read-only CLI is `node --no-maglev --import tsx scripts/seat-wardrobe-coverage.ts BUNDLE CONTRACT NEW_OUTPUT`. It refuses to overwrite receipts. Design Lab retains every run and only installs a complete receipt at `wardrobe-coverage.json`. Per-context computation caches bind renderer inventory, exact catalog loadouts, policy and unrounded pose/leg parameters; candidate aggregate receipts bind the actual settled and motion bytes. Changed source invalidates the cached computation. This body-depth prerequisite does not judge furniture ordering or appearance and cannot replace source-only pixel expectations or two local visual reviewers. Character-part publication still requires a separate staged-clothing workflow before it can make equivalent claims about newly introduced artwork.

The common publication gate applies to Design Lab, command-line publication, and direct model writes. Surface digests use the same JavaScript number serialization as the verifier, including float-rich curved maps. Source, geometry, body-reference, or renderer changes invalidate the corresponding evidence; old artifacts remain immutable research records. A status string does not override these checks.

New source annotation and boundary repairs record ownership basis and evidence for every region or pixel. `observed` remains distinct from `declared-intent`; the latter must name the supported `shared-contour` or `receiver-shadow` convention and its positive topology evidence. Unsupported soft crown/tangent boundaries stay `unresolved`. Any unresolved record or any remaining free-text uncertainty blocks generation, including cache reuse; mentioning an authoring convention does not waive an unknown. Original proposals remain diagnostic artifacts. Additive `ownership` metadata binds the exact source, orientation, physical declaration, labels, producer and convention versions, and preserves repair ancestry. This is unreviewed source authorship, never approval. Historical accepted maps remain loadable unchanged; regenerating them requires the new evidence, and a partial repair cannot retroactively certify unrecorded ownership elsewhere.

The settled review covers four explicit directions, four review outfits, and every cushion. It does not establish motion correctness or every possible custom outfit. Actual-room approach/exit films and neighboring-object ordering remain separate evidence with their own limits. Existing unchanged legacy proxies remain loadable but must never be represented as pixel-verified.

Run the free focused checks from `art/`:

```powershell
.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m unittest test_seat_curved_generate test_seat_curved_authoring test_seat_correspondence test_seat_authored_review test_designlab_surfaces test_seat_review_repair test_seat_surface_transport test_seat_visual_review
```
